📜 Our Rules

Starter community rules

  1. Har member se izzat se baat karein.
  2. Jhoothi jaankari ko fact ki tarah na failayein.
  3. Kisi ka phone number, address ya private details permission ke bina share na karein.
  4. Dhamki, harassment, nafrat aur violence ko badhava na dein.
  5. Rule proposal sabhi ko padhne aur samajhne ka mauka mile.
  6. Community rules Bharat ke samvidhan aur lagu kanoon ke adheen honge.
